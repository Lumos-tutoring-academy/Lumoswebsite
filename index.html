Questions parents ask

Something else? Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll reply within 24 hours.

Is the trial week really free?

Yes. For one week the learner joins the classes for the subjects you chose, Monday to Friday from 6 to 8pm, at no cost. We don’t ask for payment or banking details. If you’d like to stay on, you register and pay at the end of the week.

Which grades and curricula do you cover?

Grade 8 to 12, plus learners upgrading their matric results. We teach both CAPS and IEB.

How do the classes work?

Classes are live on Microsoft Teams every weekday from 6 to 8pm. We follow a set timetable each week, which we send on WhatsApp before the week starts. Learners join on a phone or laptop.

What if my child misses a class?

Every live class is recorded. Your child can watch it at their own pace before the next one.

What does the monthly fee include?

All seven subjects, the live classes and recordings, plus worksheets, learning materials and exam revision. The R100 registration fee covers setup and a baseline assessment.

Do you help with matric exam preparation?

Yes. We work through past papers, the mistakes examiners see most often, and how to set out answers to get the marks.

What happens after the free week?

We check in on WhatsApp. If you want to continue, complete the registration form and pay the R100 registration fee and your first month. If not, that’s fine, and there’s nothing to cancel.

How do I pay?

By card through PayFast, South Africa’s secure payment gateway. Your first payment covers the R100 registration fee and the first month, then PayFast charges the monthly fee automatically. We never see your card details. You can also pay by EFT: we send our banking details on WhatsApp, and EFT fees are due at the start of each month.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, by WhatsApp or email. There’s no contract. If you pay by card, we stop your PayFast subscription so you aren’t charged again. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you’ve paid for.
